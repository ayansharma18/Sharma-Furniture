Three Generations

A family business, still run by the family

Sharma Furniture started as a small workshop and has stayed one, even as the orders have grown. Every piece that leaves our workshop has been checked by someone who has a stake in the name on the door.

We don't keep a showroom stocked with finished pieces waiting to be sold. Instead, we build after you tell us what you need — your size, your choice of wood, your finish — so nothing is wasted and nothing is generic.

When you message us on WhatsApp, you're talking directly to the workshop, not a call centre. That's on purpose.
